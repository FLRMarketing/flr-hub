New accounts waiting

Nothing is switched on until you’ve checked who each person is, with contact details FLR already holds (their work number from the staff list, or Teams on their existing work account) or through IT. Never use a number, address or message that came with the sign-up.

    Accounts

    Switch an app on to let someone use it, off to take it away, and change a role from its menu. Changes apply at once, and the apps check them too, so someone can’t get in by going to an app’s address.

    What each role can do

    User

    Uses the tools switched on for them. Cost Estimator: reads every quote; creates, edits, approves, archives and exports them. Fleet Management: their own driving, once linked to their name. Annual Leave: their own leave and who’s off today. Fitter Schedule: photos and plates; the schedule itself needs the team passcode.

    Admin

    Everything a User can do, plus: approves leave and sees everyone’s, sees every driver and vehicle, sees van arrivals, and reassigns quotes. No settings.

    Super Admin

    Everything, including Hub settings (accounts, roles, apps and registration), the Cost Estimator’s prices, rules and wording, deleting quotes, and linking drivers to accounts. There’s always at least one.

    Developer

    Reads and exports quotes, with diagnostics and test data in the Cost Estimator; otherwise as a User.

    Each tool also has to be switched on for the person.
