    Serious incidents

    Recorded 20 mph or more over the speed limit.

    Check before acting on any incident

    • Speed limits come from FleetView's map data, which can be wrong, especially near limit changes and in 20 mph zones. Check the location, and dashcam footage where available, before raising an incident.
    • Incidents are recorded against a vehicle. The name shown is the FleetView-assigned driver for that vehicle. It does not prove who was driving at the time.
    • FleetView is the original record. Use "Open in FleetView" to check the event there before any conversation with a driver.
